New to this? Step by step
  1. Copy post: click the button above. The words are on your clipboard, ready to paste.
  2. Download PNG: the image saves to your Downloads folder.
  3. Share to LinkedIn: the blue button opens LinkedIn in a new tab.
  4. In LinkedIn, click Start a post.
  5. Click the photo icon and pick the image you just downloaded.
  6. Click into the text box and paste: Ctrl + V on Windows, Cmd + V on Mac.
  7. Read it back, then click Post.
